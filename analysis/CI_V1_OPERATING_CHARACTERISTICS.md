# Stage C — operating characteristics of the surface-2 dual gate

2026-09-25. **No API calls, no runs.** All numbers come from
`analysis/ci_v1/operating_characteristics.py`, which is deterministic and
re-runs in about 20 s.

How the numbers are produced:
- **Gate 2** is enumerated exactly through the product's own
  `capability.evaluate`.
- **Gate 1** is simulated, 4000 draws per cell, with an engine that
  reproduces the product's seeded bootstrap in exact integer arithmetic. The
  script checks it against `_paired_bootstrap` on 300 random cases and
  refuses to print if a single decision differs. All 300 matched.

The frozen rule is applied across n **mechanically**: eligibility is the
fraction 0.875, the warning is 0.375, and the minimum evidence is n trials.
Nothing here changes the frozen specification. These tables are what it
costs and buys.

## The answer: is n = 8 a reasonable design point?

**Yes. It is the smallest n at which gate 2 works as intended, and the
reason is structural, not a matter of degree.**

1. **At n = 5, 6 and 7 the eligibility bar demands a perfect baseline.** A
   task that is truly 0.95 is excluded from monitoring with probability
   0.23, 0.26 and 0.30 respectively. n = 8 is the first n at which one
   baseline failure is tolerated, and P(eligible) jumps to **0.94**. That is
   why power is not monotone in n. **n = 6 is worse than n = 5** (T2), and
   8 × 7 is far worse than 7 × 8 (T6).
2. **Power against a collapse, K = 7, true baseline 0.95.** A drop to 0.10
   is caught with probability 0.46 at n = 5 and **0.87 at n = 8**. A drop to
   0.25 goes from 0.18 to **0.55**.
3. **n = 10 buys a real but moderate improvement at +25% cost.** 0.95 → 0.25
   is caught with probability 0.72 instead of 0.55, and 1.00 → 0.25 with 0.92
   instead of 0.68. It also brings the smallest firing drop from 0.75 down to
   0.60 (T1). The confirmatory study grows from 224 to 280 runs and from about
   $41 to about $51 (T5).
4. **Neither gate catches moderate drops.** 1.00 → 0.75 is caught with
   probability 0.00 at n = 8 and 0.02 at n = 10. At n ≥ 8, a −0.20 shift on every
   task is caught by gate 1 only about half the time (0.44–0.65). That is by
   design: gate 2 is a **catastrophic capability regression gate**, and gate
   1 needs about −0.30 across the board for reliable detection (0.80–0.98 at
   n ≥ 8).

## Which lever is more efficient: more tasks or more repeats?

**For gate 2, more repeats, clearly.** At a fixed budget per arm (T6), 7 × 8
catches one task collapsing to 0.25 with probability 0.55, while 14 × 4 has
**0.00**. Past K ≈ 4 the Bonferroni boundary barely moves (T1), but every
extra task is a new eligibility hurdle and splits α further. Per-task
sensitivity is bought with repeats.

**For gate 1, it hardly matters at these budgets.** At 56 runs per arm,
power against −0.20 is 0.46 for 7 × 8, 0.46 for 8 × 7 and 0.41 for 14 × 4.
At 80 runs it is 0.58, 0.56 and 0.54. The bootstrap over tasks gains from K,
and per-task noise falls with n, and the two roughly cancel here.

**Not modelled:** coverage. More tasks exercise more capabilities, so a
regression is more likely to touch the suite at all. No per-task statistic
can capture that, so choosing K is partly a product decision, not a
statistical one.

## False blocks

- **Gate 2, worst case over the true rate:** at most 0.0033 per task and
  **0.013 family-wise** at any K and n tabulated (T3). The discrete exact
  test is conservative.
- **Dual gate on an unchanged candidate:**
  - realistic profiles (all 0.95, or Stage-A-like with one noisy task):
    **≤ 0.005**;
  - the adversarial profile with task rates spread evenly over 0.3–0.95:
    **≤ 0.027**.

  In every case this is under the nominal 0.05.

## What this means for the product claim

The report may say: *broad reliability — confident loss of at least 10
points across the suite; capability regression — near-collapse of a task that
reliably worked (smallest firing drop 0.75 at n = 8, K ≥ 4).* It may not say
"detects severe regressions" without the qualifier.

## Recommendation

**Keep n = 8 for the confirmatory study.** It is where gate 2 starts to work
(point 1). The study exists to falsify the rule (F1–F4). The step-40 arm is predicted to collapse
tasks to about 0/8, and a 0/8 fires whenever the task is eligible. So
detection there is essentially P(eligible): 0.94 at a true baseline of 0.95,
and 1.00 at 1.00. n = 10 is a product-default question for later,
decided on cost per PR, not on this study.
